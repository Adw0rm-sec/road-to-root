 ROAD TO ROOT — Chapter 01: "Set & Forget"
 Host: hollow-fileproc-01        Classification: CONFIDENTIAL
================================================================

OPERATOR,

Phishing got us a standard account on Hollow Industries' file-processing
box. You're on it now as 'labuser1'. This machine chews through log and
report files all day, and over the years the admins kept reaching for the
quickest fix during incidents. Quick fixes have a way of becoming permanent.

Intel from the foothold says this box leans on SUID binaries far too much.
A SUID binary runs with the file OWNER's privileges — and on this host, a
few of them are owned by root. Find them, abuse them, become root.

OBJECTIVE
  - Escalate from labuser1 to root.
  - Capture the flag:  /root/flag.txt   (only root can read it)

THERE IS MORE THAN ONE WAY IN
  We counted FIVE independent SUID paths to root on this box. You only need
  one to win — but a good operator maps them all. Try to find and exploit
  each. They teach different lessons.

FIRST MOVE
  Enumerate SUID binaries:
      find / -perm -4000 -type f 2>/dev/null
  Anything in that list owned by root is a candidate. Ask of each one:
  "If this runs as root, what can I make it do for me?"

No writeup needed in-world — but the real debrief (every path, plus how the
blue team detects and fixes each) is in  targets/suid/WRITEUP.md  once you've
had a proper go yourself.

Good hunting.
— BlackReef Command
